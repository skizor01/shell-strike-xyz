01 / The Formats.

TWO WAYS TO
PLAY SERIOUS.

Shell Strike offers two competitive formats, each built around a different team size. Classic features 2v2, 3v3 and 4v4 scrims, where teams compete in organized matches. KOTC (King of the Coop) brings larger teams into 5v5, 6v6, 7v7 and 8v8 matches.

Whether you want the precision of Classic Scrims or the chaos of KOTC, the choice is yours.
